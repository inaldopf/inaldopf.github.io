Privacy Policy

Histórias Animadas Post ("the App") is a personal tool used by a single creator to publish his own original animated videos to his own TikTok account (@historiasanimadas). Last updated: November 13, 2026.

1. Data we access

When the creator connects his TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, avatar, account identifier) and the posting options TikTok provides for the account. We do not collect data from any other TikTok user.

2. How we use the data

The data is used only to authenticate the account owner and to upload and publish videos that the account owner created and approved. We do not sell, rent, share or use the data for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely, only as long as the connection is active, and are deleted when the account owner disconnects the App or revokes access in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services).

4. Third parties

The App communicates only with TikTok's official APIs and with the hosting provider needed to run it. No data is disclosed to other third parties.

5. Your rights

You may revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings and request deletion of any stored data by contacting us.

6. Contact

Via the TikTok account @historiasanimadas.
